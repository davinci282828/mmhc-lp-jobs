The Role

What the Role Looks Like

  • Provide individual online therapy to adult clients as part of a full-time caseload, under clinical supervision.
  • Conduct intake assessments and complete timely, accurate session documentation, including SOAP notes.
  • Prepare for and participate in supervision, peer consultation, and clinical training.
  • Use client feedback and symptom measures to inform your work and discuss progress in supervision.
  • Manage appointment openings and cancellations through our platform.
  • Conduct sessions from a private, quiet, professional space in New York State.
The Ideal Candidate

Who Thrives Here

You are curious about your clients and willing to examine your own thinking. You come to supervision prepared, ask for help when you need it, and put feedback into practice.

Careful documentation, reliable follow-through, and ethical care are habits you take seriously. You want to build a steady caseload alongside colleagues who hold themselves to the same standard.

Requirements

  • A valid New York State limited permit in Mental Health Counseling, or eligibility to obtain one.
  • A master’s degree in Mental Health Counseling.
  • Residence in New York State.
  • Clear writing, strong organizational habits, and reliable follow-through.
  • A commitment to ethical client care and continued clinical development.
